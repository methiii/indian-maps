    Every record shows how far it has travelled through the system

    An audit objection is not a charge, and a charge is not a verdict. CivicLens labels every item with the stage the public record has reached, and shows the date of the last update.

    Words used

    • Accused under FIR No. [X]
    • Remanded to judicial custody
    • Facing charges under Section [Y]
    • Shared corporate identifier, for verification

    Words not used

    • Culprit
    • Criminal
    • Corrupt
    • Racketeer
    1. Audit objectionCAG or Local Fund Audit notes irregular spending or missing vouchers.
    2. Preliminary vigilance inquiryLokayukta or Anti-Corruption Bureau verification. No formal charge.
    3. FIR or criminal caseRegistered under the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988.
    4. Cognisance and charges framedA court has examined the chargesheet and framed charges.
    5. Trial outcomeConviction, or acquittal or discharge. Shown with equal prominence.
    6. Asset recoveryDocumented return of public money under attachment orders.

    Rules the platform is being built to follow

    These are design commitments from the project plan. Each one needs a working process and a legal review before the site publishes real case records.

    Victim and juvenile identities are never published
    Sexual-offence and child cases are masked, and locations are widened to sub-district level (Section 72 BNS, Section 23 POCSO Act, Juvenile Justice Act).
    Named people and companies can reply
    A right-of-reply form will attach verified responses to the record within 48 hours. The form is not built yet.
    Outcomes are corrected as prominently as allegations
    When a case ends in acquittal, discharge or quashing, the record updates within 24 hours with the same visual weight.
    Names are matched on identifiers, not spelling
    Links between a person and a company need a PAN, DIN or CIN. Uncertain matches go to a human analyst first.
    Gaps are shown, not hidden
    A score is withheld when verified data covers less than 65% of its parts, and the missing feed is listed under Data status.

    What this site will not do

    • Rank cities as best or worst, or blend everything into one score
    • Show crowd-sourced, unverified crime pins
    • Predict crime or profile neighbourhoods
    • Use facial recognition or CCTV feeds
    • Publish sealed FIRs or victim details
    • Conclude that anyone is guilty of corruption

    Start with two places, then widen

    Phase 1: pilot

    Months 1 to 2. Delhi NCT and Pune district.

    • Live air quality
    • Daily MGNREGA figures
    • Candidate disclosures (Form 26)
    • Manual 7-day serious-crime briefing
    • Eight-part scorecard prototype

    Phase 2: expansion

    Months 3 to 6. Maharashtra and Uttar Pradesh.

    • Automated tender parsing and single-bid flags
    • Court case tracking
    • Contractor network checks
    • RTI tracker
    • Burking sensitivity audit

    Phase 3: national

    Months 7 to 18. Larger cities and flagged states.

    • Ward-level inequality maps
    • Live infrastructure trackers
    • Conflict-of-interest graph
    • Open data API

    CivicLens is a prototype built from the Civic Intelligence and Accountability Platform plan. It publishes structure and method first. It does not yet publish verified records about any person or company.

    Registration of an FIR or the start of an inquiry does not establish guilt. Every person named in a public record is presumed innocent until a court decides otherwise.
